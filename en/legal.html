Refund and Cancellation Policy

Our passengers' safety is our priority. Because balloon flights depend entirely on weather conditions, the following policies apply:

  • Cancellation by the operator: If the pilot decides to cancel the flight because of adverse weather or safety reasons, the passenger is entitled to move the flight to another available date within the following 12 months or receive a full refund of the amount paid.
  • Refunds during the first 15 days: You can request cancellation and a full refund, without fees, during the 15 calendar days following purchase, provided that the flight has not taken place. This applies to all purchases, including dated bookings, open tickets and gift tickets. Email info@voyagerballoons.eu with your order reference to request it. You do not need to return any physical product.
  • Cancellation or changes by the customer:
    • Date changes are permitted only in cases of force majeure, upon presentation of supporting documentation.
    • Once the refund period of 15 calendar days from purchase has expired, and except in the cases above, cancellations, changes and refunds requested by the customer will not be accepted.
    • Once that refund period has expired, if the passenger does not attend the flight (no-show), no refund will be issued and the ticket cannot be rescheduled; the amount paid will be forfeited.
  • Gift tickets do not expire. A refund can be requested during the 15 calendar days following purchase if the flight has not taken place. The gift recipient must contact us on WhatsApp at 614 007 056 with the ticket reference and their preferred dates. Our team checks availability and confirms the booking; buying a ticket does not reserve a date or seats.

Disclaimer and Health Requirements

Balloon flight is a safe activity, but requires a minimum level of health. By making a booking, the customer accepts and declares:

  • That they do not have serious health problems, heart conditions, or conditions affecting the spine, neck or bones that would make light impacts inadvisable.
  • That female passengers are not pregnant. Safety rules strictly prohibit pregnant women from flying.
  • That they understand that landing may involve light impacts or dragging of the basket.
  • That they meet the minimum weight/size and age requirements for safe flight.
